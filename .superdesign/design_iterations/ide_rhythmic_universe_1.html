Sound Waves & Frequency

Sound is created by vibrations traveling through air. The frequency of these vibrations determines pitch - faster vibrations create higher pitches, slower vibrations create lower pitches.

⚡

Resonance

When an object vibrates at its natural frequency, it resonates, amplifying the sound. Instrument bodies are designed to resonate at specific frequencies to enhance tone.

🎵

Harmonics & Overtones

Musical notes contain multiple frequencies - the fundamental and harmonics. This harmonic content gives each instrument its unique timbre and character.

📊

Equal Temperament

The tuning system that divides the octave into 12 equal semitones, allowing instruments to play together in any key. This system underlies modern Western music.

🎶

Scales & Intervals

Specific patterns of intervals between notes create scales. These mathematical relationships produce the emotional qualities we associate with different modes and keys.

🔄

Standing Waves

In wind and string instruments, standing wave patterns determine which pitches can be produced. The length and thickness of strings or air columns affect these patterns.

About Rhythmic Universe

Rhythmic Universe explores the profound connections between cosmic phenomena and musical expression. From the rotation of planets to the vibration of strings, rhythm governs both the celestial and the sonic.

Ancient civilizations understood that music and mathematics were reflections of universal order. Modern science reveals that the same wave principles governing sound also describe light, radio waves, and even quantum mechanics.

This cosmic perspective illuminates how every musical instrument participates in the grand symphony of the universe, following the same fundamental laws that govern stellar formation and galactic motion.

Journey through this rhythmic universe to discover how earthly music connects us to the infinite patterns and frequencies that shape our cosmos.
