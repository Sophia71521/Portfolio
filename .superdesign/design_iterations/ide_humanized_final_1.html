Did You Know?

Fascinating facts about our cosmic neighborhood

A Day on Venus is Longer Than Its Year

Venus takes 243 Earth days to rotate once on its axis, but only 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun. This means a Venusian day is actually longer than a Venusian year!

You Could Fit All the Planets Between Earth and the Moon

The Moon is about 384,400 km from Earth. If you lined up all the planets in our solar system, they would fit in that space with room to spare! The total diameter of all planets is about 380,016 km.

Saturn Would Float in Water

Saturn is the least dense planet in our solar system. Its density is actually less than water (0.687 g/cm³), which means if you could find a bathtub big enough, Saturn would float!

Space Facts & Explorer

Learn fascinating facts about our solar system

⚡

Light Speed

Light travels at 299,792 km/s. It takes sunlight 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth, but over 4 hours to reach Neptune!

🌌

Astronomical Unit

1 AU (Astronomical Unit) is the distance from Earth to the Sun: 149,597,871 kilometers. It's our cosmic measuring stick for the solar system.

🪐

Saturn's Rings

Saturn's rings are made of billions of pieces of ice and rock, some as small as grains of sand, others as large as mountains.

🌍

Earth's Gravity

Earth's gravity is 9.81 m/s². On Jupiter, you'd weigh 2.5 times more. On Mars, only 38% of your Earth weight!

🌙

Moon Facts

The Moon is slowly drifting away from Earth at about 3.8 cm per year. In the distant past, it was much closer and appeared larger in the sky!

☄️

Asteroid Belt

The asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter contains millions of rocky objects, but if you combined them all, they'd form a body smaller than Earth's moon.
